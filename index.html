Full-Time Leadership Program
IRG NextGen Leaders

The IRG NextGen Leaders Program is a full-time paid leadership development program for culinary, food & beverage, and multi-unit operations professionals. Participants train within the IRG Legacy Leadership Institute™ — IRG's own proprietary leadership curriculum spanning ten pillars and four certification levels — while building the role-specific fundamentals required to lead at their level.

Participants are placed on the Level 2, 3, or 4 certification track based on their target role and progress toward a certified leadership placement at program completion.

Who Can Apply

  • Recent graduates (within 24 months) with a degree in Hospitality, Business, Culinary Arts, Communications, or any related field
  • Hospitality professionals with 2+ years of direct restaurant or hospitality experience (no degree required)
  • Current IRG team members with a formal promotion or written manager referral
  • Must be authorized to work in the United States

Paid College Internship
Iconic Future Leaders Internship

The Iconic Future Leaders Internship is a 12-week paid experience for college students built on the IRG Legacy Leadership Institute™ Level 1 curriculum — the same foundational framework every new IRG restaurant manager completes. Interns work real shifts in a chosen track, attend monthly leadership development sessions, and complete a Leadership Impact Capstone project.

Strong performers are eligible to convert directly into the IRG NextGen Leaders Program upon graduation — no re-application required. Academic credit is available with full IRG documentation support.

Who Can Apply

  • Currently enrolled in an accredited college or university
  • Sophomore standing or above preferred
  • Available for a minimum of 24 hours per week
  • Genuine interest in the hospitality industry
  • Must be authorized to work in the United States
